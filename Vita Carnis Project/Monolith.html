ANOMALY #07 — MONOLITH

THREAT LEVEL: EXTREME (UNKNOWN INTENT)

CLEARANCE LEVEL: RESTRICTED — LEVEL 4 AND ABOVE

ANATOMICAL & BEHAVIORAL PROFILE:

Coelum Carnis, referred to as "Monoliths," are colossal entities reaching heights over 120 meters. Composed of dark, stone-like biological plating, they stand in motionless, precise circular formations across designated remote geographical sectors.

BEHAVIOR:

Monoliths exhibit no active hunting behavior or physical movement. However, they radiate high-frequency electromagnetic pulses and appear to act as relays or beacons for the broader Vita Carnis network.
