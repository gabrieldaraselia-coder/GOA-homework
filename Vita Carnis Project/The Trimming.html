ANOMALY #02 — THE TRIMMING

THREAT LEVEL: MINIMAL

RESTRICTED — LEVEL 1 AND ABOVE

ANATOMICAL & BEHAVIORAL PROFILE:

Ignavus Carnis, commonly known as "The Trimming," is a small, limbless quadrupedal organism and the most basic creature generated by Crawl Nodes. Visually resembling a small blob of raw meat with rudimentary facial features, it possesses low intelligence and displays zero natural aggression toward humans.

BEHAVIOR:

Trimmings act as scavengers, feeding on organic waste and small vegetation. Due to their docile nature, domestic encounters are common. However, citizens are advised against domestication; unmonitored Trimmings can mature or attract larger predators.
