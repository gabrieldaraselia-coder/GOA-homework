ANOMALY #04 — THE MIMIC

THREAT LEVEL: HIGH (DEADLY)

CLEARANCE LEVEL: RESTRICTED — LEVEL 3 AND ABOVE

ANATOMICAL & BEHAVIORAL PROFILE:

Trucidatum Carnis, or "The Mimic," is a bipedal apex predator specifically evolved to hunt human beings. Its skin resembles pale, stretched human tissue, and its elongated limbs allow for extreme mobility and stealth.

BEHAVIOR & TACTICS:

Mimics display advanced spatial awareness and ambush tactics. They enter human dwellings, blending into shadows or mimicking structural shapes (furniture, doorway silhouettes) to remain unnoticed until the victim is isolated.

EVOLUTIONARY VARIANT — ELDER MIMIC:

Mimics that survive extended lifespans undergo significant mutation into Elder Mimics. They feature hardened muscular armor, enhanced speed, and severe resistance to standard small-arms fire.
