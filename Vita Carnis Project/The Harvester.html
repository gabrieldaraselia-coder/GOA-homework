ANOMALY #05 — THE HARVESTER

THREAT LEVEL: SEVERE

CLEARANCE LEVEL: RESTRICTED — LEVEL 3 AND ABOVE

ANATOMICAL & BEHAVIORAL PROFILE:

Congregentur Carnis, designated "The Harvester," is a stationary, subterranean predator. Its central body remains buried underground, while a dense network of razor-thin, tendril-like vines extends across the surrounding surface soil.

BEHAVIOR & HUNTING MECHANISM:

When a target steps on the hidden tendrils, the organism fires barbed filaments into the victim's flesh, injecting a powerful neurotoxin that causes immediate paralysis. The target is then dragged beneath the soil for slow digestion over several days.
