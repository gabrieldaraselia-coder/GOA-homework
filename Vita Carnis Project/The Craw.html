ANOMALY #01 — THE CRAWL

THREAT LEVEL: LOW (DIRECT) / CRITICAL (ECOLOGICAL)

CLEARANCE LEVEL: RESTRICTED — LEVEL 2 AND ABOVE

ANATOMICAL & BEHAVIORAL PROFILE:

Scandere Carnis, colloquially designated as "The Crawl," is a fibrous, vascular organism that functions as the foundational bedrock for all known Vita Carnis life forms. Visually resembling a network of exposed human veins, arterial tissue, and fibrous muscle strands, it spreads invasively across porous surfaces, subterranean structures, and internal building foundations.Unlike other anomalies in the database, The Crawl exhibits zero locomotive capability and lacks predatory aggression toward human targets. It operates passively, absorbing ambient organic nutrients and moisture from its surrounding environment to maintain expansion.

CONTAINMENT NOTE:

Direct mechanical severance or incineration of active Crawl tissue triggers a violent release of localized airborne spores and biochemical distress signals, which may draw hostile organisms (Mimics, Harvesters) to the breach site. Any localized outbreak must be reported to C.A.R.C.A.S. containment squads immediately. Do not attempt self-clearing.
