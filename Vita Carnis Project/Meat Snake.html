ANOMALY #03 — MEAT SNAKE

THREAT LEVEL: LOW / MODERATE

RESTRICTED — LEVEL 2 AND ABOVE

ANATOMICAL & BEHAVIORAL PROFILE:

Mutuari Carnis, designated as the "Meat Snake," is an elongated, serpentine anomaly composed of dense muscular tissue. It lacks internal skeletal structures of its own; instead, it scavenges skulls from animal or human carcasses to protect its vulnerable head segment.

BEHAVIOR:

It operates primarily as a subterranean scavenger, consuming decaying organic matter. While generally non-confrontational, Meat Snakes will defend themselves if cornered or if their acquired protective skull is threatened.
