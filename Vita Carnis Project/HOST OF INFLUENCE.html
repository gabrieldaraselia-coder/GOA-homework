ANOMALY #06 — HOST OF INFLUENCE

THREAT LEVEL: SEVERE (PSYCHOLOGICAL / COGNITIVE)

CLEARANCE LEVEL: RESTRICTED — LEVEL 4 AND ABOVE

ANATOMICAL & BEHAVIORAL PROFILE:

Imperium Carnis is a tall, humanoid structure anchored to the ground by heavy fungal-like root systems. It continuously emits airborne microscopic spores capable of infiltrating human respiratory systems.

BEHAVIOR & HAZARD:

Prolonged exposure to these spores induces severe auditory hallucinations, cognitive degradation, and a compulsion to walk directly into the organism's central mass, where victims are absorbed to sustain its spore production.
